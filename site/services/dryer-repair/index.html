Burning smell? Stop the dryer.

If you smell burning or the dryer is unusually hot, turn it off and unplug it. Lint buildup in the vent is a leading cause of home dryer fires. Don't keep running it while you wait for a repair.

Quick checks before you call

These take a few minutes and sometimes solve the problem. If they don't, you've ruled out the easy stuff, which helps the diagnosis.

  • Clean the lint screen. A clogged screen alone can double drying time.
  • Check the vent hose behind the dryer for crushing or kinks, and make sure the outside vent flap opens when the dryer runs.
  • Electric dryers use a double breaker. If one half trips, the drum can spin with no heat, so check the panel.

Dust and long vent runs

Desert dust and monsoon storms add to lint buildup, and many Arizona homes vent dryers up through the roof with long runs. Restricted airflow is one of the most common reasons a dryer takes forever or overheats.

Common questions

Why does my dryer run but not heat?

Common causes are a failed heating element or thermal fuse on electric dryers, a bad igniter or gas valve coils on gas dryers, or a half-tripped breaker. Perri will find the actual cause before replacing anything.

Do you repair gas dryers?

Yes, both gas and electric dryers.

Can a clogged vent damage my dryer?

Yes. Restricted airflow makes the dryer run hot, which can blow thermal fuses and wear out heating parts early. It's also a fire risk.
