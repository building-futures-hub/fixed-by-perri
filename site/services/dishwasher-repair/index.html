Quick checks before you call

These take a few minutes and sometimes solve the problem. If they don't, you've ruled out the easy stuff, which helps the diagnosis.

  • Pull out and rinse the filter in the bottom of the tub. A clogged filter is a top cause of poor cleaning and slow draining.
  • If you just installed a new garbage disposal, the knockout plug in the disposal's dishwasher inlet may never have been removed.
  • Run the kitchen faucet until it's hot before starting a cycle.
  • Check the spray arm holes for food or mineral buildup.

Hard water and dishwashers

That white film on your glasses is usually hard water minerals. Rinse aid helps, but over time scale also builds up in spray arms, valves and heating parts, which can cause poor cleaning and drain problems.

Common questions

Why won't my dishwasher drain?

Usually a clogged filter, a blocked drain hose or air gap, a disposal issue, or a failing drain pump. Perri checks the simple causes first.

Is a leaking dishwasher urgent?

A slow leak can damage cabinets and subfloor without being obvious. If you see water, stop using it and turn off the water supply under the sink until it's checked.

Which dishwasher brands do you repair?

Most major brands, including Bosch, Samsung, LG, Whirlpool and GE.
