Quick checks before you call

These take a few minutes and sometimes solve the problem. If they don't, you've ruled out the easy stuff, which helps the diagnosis.

  • Check the drain hose for kinks, especially if the washer was recently moved.
  • Front-loaders have a drain pump filter (coin trap) behind a small panel at the bottom front. A sock or coin there is a common cause of drain problems.
  • For shaking, make sure the load is balanced and the washer sits level on all four feet.
  • For fill problems, confirm both water valves behind the washer are fully open.

Hard water is hard on washers

Mineral scale from central Arizona's hard water builds up in inlet screens, valves and heating parts over time. If your washer fills slowly or you see white residue, scale is a likely suspect.

Common questions

Should I repair or replace my washer?

Many washer problems (drain pumps, lid switches, door locks, belts, inlet valves) are very fixable. You'll get the diagnosis and price before work starts, so you can decide.

Do you work on front-load and top-load washers?

Yes, both, from most major brands including Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE and Bosch.

Why does my front-load washer smell?

Moisture trapped in the door gasket and drain area grows mildew. Wiping the gasket, leaving the door cracked between loads, and using HE detergent help. If the smell persists, the drain filter or pump may need attention.
