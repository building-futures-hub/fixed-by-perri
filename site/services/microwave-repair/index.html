Microwave problems we fix

  • Runs but doesn't heat food
  • Sparking or arcing inside
  • No power or dead display
  • Turntable not spinning
  • Buttons or keypad not responding
  • Light or vent fan not working
  • Door won't latch or start
Don't open the case yourself.

Microwaves store dangerous high voltage in internal parts even when unplugged. If it's sparking or smells burnt, unplug it and leave the repair to a technician.

Quick checks before you call

These take a few minutes and sometimes solve the problem. If they don't, you've ruled out the easy stuff, which helps the diagnosis.

  • Check the breaker and outlet first.
  • Make sure the turntable and its roller ring are seated correctly.
  • Never run a microwave empty, and remove any metal, foil or twist ties.

Common questions

Is it worth fixing a microwave?

Over-the-range and built-in microwaves are often worth repairing because replacement means installation work too. A small countertop microwave is usually cheaper to replace, and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case.

Why is my microwave sparking?

Usually metal in the cavity, food splatter, or a damaged waveguide cover. Stop using it until it's checked.

Do you repair over-the-range microwaves?

Yes, over-the-range and built-in models from most major brands.
