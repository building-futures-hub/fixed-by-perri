Smell gas? Leave first, call second.

Don't use light switches, the stove or your phone inside. Get everyone out of the house, then call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Call us once the gas company has made it safe.

Quick checks before you call

These take a few minutes and sometimes solve the problem. If they don't, you've ruled out the easy stuff, which helps the diagnosis.

  • On a gas cooktop, make sure each burner cap is seated correctly and completely dry after cleaning.
  • Check the breaker. Electric ranges run on a double breaker, and half can trip.
  • If the display shows an error code, write it down. It helps speed up the diagnosis.

Common questions

Do you repair gas ranges?

Yes. We repair both gas and electric ranges, cooktops and wall ovens.

My oven temperature seems off. Can that be fixed?

Often, yes. A failing temperature sensor, bake or broil element, or igniter can cause slow or uneven heating. We'll test and confirm the cause.

Is it safe to use my oven while it's acting up?

If you smell gas, see sparking, or the oven won't shut off, stop using it and call. For slow heating or uneven baking, it's usually safe to use carefully until the repair.
