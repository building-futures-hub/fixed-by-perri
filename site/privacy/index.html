Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

Fixed by Perri ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a service request, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, and a description of your appliance problem. Like most websites, our hosting provider may also log basic technical information such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, send appointment reminders and follow-ups, and improve our service. We may contact you by phone, text message or email about your request.

Text messages

If you provide your phone number, you may receive text messages related to your service request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. We do not sell or share your mobile number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example, our scheduling and communication tools), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing fixedbyperri@gmail.com or calling (520) 954-5559.
